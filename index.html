How it works

Your bins are already empty and already at the curb after Friday's pickup. That's the whole trick.

STEP 1

Book a slot

Pick one of the four Saturday spots below and add your address in The Grove and which bins you want cleaned.

STEP 2

Leave them out

After Friday's pickup, leave your empty bins at the curb overnight, and make sure I can get to your outdoor hose and power outlet.

STEP 3

Saturday power wash

Saturday morning I power wash each can inside and out with soap and degreaser.

STEP 4

Back by your garage

Clean bins get rolled back up your driveway, drip-dried and smelling fresh.

Why bother?

A summer week in a closed plastic bin turns leftovers into a smell you can't miss from the garage. A clean can keeps flies, maggots, and raccoons away, and makes taking out the trash a lot less gross.

Questions

How do I pay?

$15 per clean, paid in cash or by Venmo to @NVD-17.

What if I forget to leave my bins out?

No problem. I'll skip your house that Saturday and catch you next time. Just let me know if you want a make-up clean.

What do I need to provide?

Just access to an outdoor hose spigot and an outdoor power outlet. I bring the power washer, soap, degreaser, and extension cord.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as I can reach your outdoor hose and outlet and the outlet has power.
